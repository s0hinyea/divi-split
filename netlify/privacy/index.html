Privacy Policy
Effective date: June 1, 2026

Divi ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Divi mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account and use Divi:

  • Account information: your name and email address
  • Payment handles: your Venmo username, Cash App tag, and/or Zelle phone number or email, which you optionally provide so friends can pay you
  • Bill and transaction data: items, amounts, and splits you create within the app
  • Contacts: with your permission, we access your device contacts solely to help you assign bill items to friends by name. Contact data is not stored on our servers.
  • Push notification token: to send you payment reminders and confirmations
  • Device information: basic device and OS identifiers used for app functionality and error reporting

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Divi account
  • To generate and share payment request links with the people you choose
  • To send you push notifications about your payment requests
  • To allow contacts you invite to view and pay their share
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Payment Processing

Divi does not process or handle any financial transactions. All payments are made directly between users through third-party services (Venmo, Cash App, Zelle). Divi only stores the payment handles you choose to add to your profile so others can find and pay you. We are not responsible for any transactions made through those platforms.

Third-Party Services

Divi uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase - database and authentication infrastructure. Your account data is stored securely on Supabase servers.
  • Expo - push notification delivery. Your device push token is used solely to deliver notifications from Divi.

These providers have their own privacy policies governing their use of data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the Divi app (Settings > Delete Account). Upon deletion, your account information, payment handles, and transaction history are permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Divi is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
