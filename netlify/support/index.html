FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Divi work?
Divi lets you split a bill with friends. Add the items, assign who owes what, and send each person a payment request link. They can pay you directly through Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle.
Does Divi process payments?
No. Divi does not handle any money. All payments happen directly between you and your friends through Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle. Divi just tracks who owes what.
How do I add my Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle?
Go to your Profile in the app and tap Edit. You can add your payment handles there so friends can pay you directly.
A friend received a payment link but the amount looks wrong.
The person who created the bill can edit it from within the Divi app before the request is settled. If the bill has already been paid, please reach out to us directly.
How do I delete my account?
Open the Divi app, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data within 30 days.
I didn't receive my verification email.
Check your spam or junk folder. If it's not there, try resending the verification from the app or contact us at helpdivi@outlook.com.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Your data is stored securely and never sold. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
